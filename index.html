MASQUIEL

False Prophet of Emris
Overview
Masquiel believes himself chosen by the god Emris to interpret divine signs and guide others toward sacred purpose. Through dreams and whispers carried by the sea, he records visions that he believes come from the heavens. Yet the voice guiding him may not belong to Emris at all.
Character Information
Role: Cultist / Prophet
Faith: Emris
Status: Receiving Visions
Alignment: Unknown
Religious Practices
• Records visions in a personal manuscript
• Observes the ocean for divine signs
• Performs rituals at night
• Spreads the word of Emris
Recorded Visions
"The ocean whispers your purpose."
"The island will become sacred ground."
"The faithful must prepare."
Hidden Truth
The voice Masquiel follows may belong to another god entirely. A being that hides behind the name of Emris and slowly guides the prophet toward unknown goals.
