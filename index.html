Overview
The Prophet's Account

Masquiel believes himself chosen by the god Emris to interpret divine signs. Through dreams, whispers carried on the tides, and the endless rhythm of the sea, he records prophecies he is certain come from the heavens above Coral Island.

He arrived on the island among the others — ordinary in every visible way. What separates him is the voice. It began as faint murmuring beneath the waves. It grew into something more.

He does not question it. He records it. He believes.

Yet the voice he follows may not belong to Emris at all.

The Hidden Truth
✗
Known only to the heavens

The voice Masquiel hears does not belong to Emris.

Emris created the world — shaped the ocean and the island, breathed life into creatures and land. He watches his creation from above with something like care.

But Emris did not choose Masquiel. The voice that calls itself divine, that feeds him visions and whispers purpose into his waking hours — it belongs to something else entirely. Something that has learned to wear the name of a god like borrowed clothing.

Masquiel has yet to realize the deception. His faith is genuine. His devotion is real. That is precisely what makes him useful.

The True Source
Pring
A shapeshifter. Created incomplete and hidden away in the world, shaped by no one fully. He carries hatred and vengeance where his form was never finished. He does not announce himself. He finds those who are already listening — and gives them something to hear.
