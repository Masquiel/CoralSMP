MASQUIEL

False Prophet of Emris
Overview
Masquiel believes himself chosen by the god Emris to interpret divine signs. Through dreams, whispers, and the endless rhythm of the sea, he records prophecies he believes come from the heavens. Yet the voice guiding him may not belong to Emris at all.
Character Information
Role: Cultist / Prophet
Faith: Emris
Status: Receiving Visions
Alignment: Unknown
Religious Practices
• Records visions in a personal manuscript
• Watches the ocean for divine signs
• Performs rituals during the quiet hours of night
• Spreads the word of Emris

Hidden Truth
The voice Masquiel hears may belong to another god entirely. A being that hides behind the name of Emris and slowly shapes the prophet's path for its own unknown purpose. Masquiel has yet to realize the deception.
